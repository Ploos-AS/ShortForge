# M2.3 — Audio mixing and burned captions

M2.3 upgrades the FFmpeg render plan from video-only assembly to a complete audiovisual short.

Ready voice, music and SFX assets are placed according to manifest start times. Music is mixed at 0.22 gain, SFX at 0.55 and voice at unity, then the combined mix is limited before AAC encoding.

Captions are burned into the video with manifest timing and centered at 78% of frame height, keeping text away from the lowest vertical UI region. Text is escaped before being inserted into the FFmpeg filter graph.

The render plan exposes feature flags for captions and audio mixing. Plan-only mode remains deterministic and does not require FFmpeg.
