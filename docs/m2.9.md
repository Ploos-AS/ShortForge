# M2.9 — YouTube finishing

M2.9 extends the qualified M2.8 video upload without broadening privileges unnecessarily.

- After a successful resumable video upload, ShortForge uploads `thumbnail.png` with `thumbnails.set`.
- The existing `youtube.upload` scope is sufficient for custom thumbnails.
- Publishing packages generate a timed `captions.srt` sidecar when timed caption assets are present.
- Caption upload planning is separate and declares `youtube.force-ssl`, because YouTube requires that stronger scope for `captions.insert`.
- ShortForge does not use YouTube's deprecated caption `sync` parameter.
- Caption network transport remains disabled until its multipart upload path has dedicated qualification.

This keeps normal video+thumbnail publishing least-privilege while making caption requirements explicit.
