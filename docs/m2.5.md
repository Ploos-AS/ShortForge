# M2.5 — Production profiles and platform QA

M2.5 separates platform-facing production policy from the FFmpeg assembler.

Profiles:
- `generic-vertical`
- `tiktok`
- `reels`
- `shorts`

All current profiles render ShortForge's preferred delivery master at 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC. Platform-specific safe zones are conservative ShortForge advisory defaults, not claims of exact UI geometry.

The Shorts profile enforces a 180-second maximum. This reflects current YouTube eligibility for square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes. Other platform duration limits are intentionally not hard-coded until an appropriate stable requirement is established.

Usage:

```sh
shortforge render build/my-short --production-profile tiktok
```

The selected profile and safe-zone metadata are recorded in the render plan, making downstream QA and publishing automation inspectable.
