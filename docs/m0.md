# ShortForge M0

## Objective

Represent a complete short-form-video plan and score candidate variants before committing to expensive generation.

## M0 deliverables

1. Short project manifest.
2. Beat/timeline model.
3. Hook, escalation, payoff and loop metadata.
4. Comedy-mode metadata.
5. Caption and audio cues.
6. Shot plan.
7. Provider-neutral generation requests.
8. Variant/cut representation.
9. Review/scoring model.
10. CLI contract for M1.

## Proposed CLI

```text
shortforge init <project>
shortforge validate <project>
shortforge ideate <project>
shortforge script <project>
shortforge score <project>
shortforge shots <project>
shortforge prompts <project>
shortforge render <project>
shortforge cuts <project>
```

## Suggested scoring dimensions

- hook strength
- clarity
- novelty
- comedic payoff
- pacing
- retention risk
- rewatch/loop quality
- caption readability
- production feasibility

Scores guide iteration; they are not treated as guarantees of platform performance.
